# Question bank and study tools

Aster now contains 1,036 original and classroom-review multiple-choice questions across 13 subjects. These are general subject practice, not official Cambridge/IB questions or a complete examination syllabus.

| Mathematics | 267 |

The bank combines concept recognition, explanation recall, application scenarios, and mathematically generated worked problems. Paired recall prompts share a concept identifier so sessions spread them out where possible. Maths contains number, powers, fractions, ratio, algebra, geometry, statistics, probability, and gradient practice; science includes investigation, biology, chemistry, and physics.

Students choose 5, 10, 20, or 30 questions, topic, recall/application, and new-first/mixed/missed selection. New-first and missed modes use the latest recorded answer to each question. The existing history retains the last 500 attempts; older checks outside that history can become eligible as new again. A filtered session can be shorter than requested when fewer questions match. Flashcards use 20-card sets; the knowledge map initially renders 24 questions and supports loading more.

Original additions have no teacher-source attribution. Existing classroom items retain their unit links. The practice screen links the distinction to the separately sourced official-paper library.

## Exam Coach quality boundary

The local vision model successfully transcribed a synthetic exam image, but testing also found incorrect reasoning and an AI-generated multiple-choice question with no correct option. Transcription, evidence, and selected focus areas must be checked by the student. Model confidence is labelled as an AI estimate.

For confirmed simple linear-equation mistakes in local mode, targeted questions are selected from the checked original algebra bank. Other topics can use model-generated questions and are explicitly labelled accordingly. A deterministic check rejects simple numeric linear-equation questions with no unique correct option and replaces a wrong answer key/explanation when the equation is supported. This is a narrow check, not a general proof of AI accuracy. OpenAI remains disabled without explicit local configuration and sharing consent.

## Related additions

- Research desk: real outbound Google, Google Scholar, or YouTube searches; internal filtering and bookmarks for 74 official paper collections (274 resource links) and 58 video lessons/collections. Embedded videos connect only after Load video is pressed.
- Reading room: six original passages with 18 objective questions and 12 written responses, explanations, and example answers. Written work is not automatically graded as an official exam.
- Writing studio: browser-saved essays and slides, editable Word and PowerPoint exports. Signed-in workspace snapshots sync to the local MySQL account store.

## Verification

The automated suite validates bank uniqueness, four distinct choices, explanations, algebra/fraction distractors, session filtering, targeted retries, local/cloud gating, and supported exam-question checks. Browser checks cover a complete five-question algebra session, saved missed-question selection, 20-card flashcard sizing, mobile layout, dark mode, official-paper dialogs, and video loading consent. Exported DOCX/PPTX files were inspected as Office XML, including styled text, editable slides, and speaker notes.
