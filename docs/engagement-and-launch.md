# Aster: engagement and launch decisions

Implemented 20 September 2026.

## Learning engagement

The goal is repeatable, useful study, measured by completed practice and learning progress. More time on screen by itself is not the goal.

- A learner chooses a weekly goal of 2, 3, or 5 study days. A completed review or saved focus minute counts. The seven-day view uses actual saved activity, includes rest days, and does not remove earned work after an absence.
- The home stopwatch begins at zero and has pause and finish controls. The countdown room remains an option. A finished session encourages a break.
- Existing retrieval questions provide explanatory feedback. The quiz records confidence and the study lab offers mistake repair. These are practice mechanisms, not guarantees of achievement.
- Rewards are predictable: 2 coins per correct review answer, 50 coins per recorded percentage exam strictly above 90%, 4 coins per video-game session. The exam key persists separately from the editable grade list to avoid repeat bonuses after reimport.
- A companion can be adopted once and customised. Food triggers a short animation. No starvation, care penalties, random loot boxes, financial coin sales, or loss threats are implemented.
- Reduced-motion settings and the quieter Studio appearance remain available.

Evidence informed these design choices; it does not establish an effect size for Aster:

- [EEF evidence review on metacognition and self-regulation](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/evidence-reviews/metacognition-and-self-regulation): supports helping learners plan, monitor, and evaluate their work.
- [The role of variable retrieval in effective learning, PNAS](https://doi.org/10.1073/PNAS.2413511121): experimental evidence relevant to retrieval-based practice. Aster’s particular implementation has not been evaluated in that study.

## Preview boundaries

Verification codes are generated by the local API and checked with expiration, attempt limits, single use, and a hashed challenge. The code is intentionally displayed in the browser for a demo. It is never represented as an email/SMS send or verified contact ownership. No delivery provider is connected.

Pro is a local UI preview, toggled by the user. It is not a secure commercial entitlement or a subscription. No card fields, payments, or renewals exist. Production billing would require a payment provider, server-side entitlements, and verified provider events. Coins and adoption are local progress state, not a fraud-resistant financial system.

Optional owner insights and individual summary sharing are separate, default-off server preferences. The owner endpoint requires a locally granted database role. Aggregated review/focus totals are withheld below five participating accounts. This reduces small-group exposure; it does not make the source records anonymous. No account is made an owner simply by registering.

## Items needed before public launch

- Confirm the legal operator, service address, launch countries, and student age policy.
- Resolve parent/guardian or school authorisation where required. The current preview does not verify age or guardian permission.
- Complete final terms and a processing-purpose/legal-basis inventory, including retention, backup erasure, request handling, processors, and any transfers.
- Configure verification delivery and finalise abuse prevention and account recovery.
- Connect billing and server-side entitlement checks; finalise tax-inclusive pricing, renewal, cancellation, withdrawal, and refund information.
- Configure hosting, HTTPS, operational monitoring, moderation, and an actual support process.
- Grant the operator role only to a specifically identified account, through local administration.
